NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of June 2, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Helios Robotics,
Inc. ("Discloser") and Ironwood Data Partners LLC ("Recipient") in connection with Discloser's disclosure of
information to Recipient for the purpose of evaluating a data-processing engagement (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any technical, business, or financial
information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, whether oral, written, or electronic, and whether or not marked
confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

2. Exclusions. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of Recipient, was
already known to Recipient, is independently developed without reference to Discloser's information, or is
rightfully received from a third party.

3. Use and Standard of Care. Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and protect it
with reasonable care, and shall limit disclosure to employees with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality
obligations no less protective than those herein.

4. Compelled Disclosure. Recipient required by law to disclose Confidential Information shall give prompt notice
before disclosure, where legally permitted, and cooperate with Discloser's efforts to seek protective treatment.

5. Term and Confidentiality Period. This Agreement shall remain in effect for one (1) year from the Effective Date,
and all confidentiality obligations hereunder shall terminate six (6) months after the termination or expiration of
this Agreement.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon termination or Discloser's written request, Recipient shall return or destroy all
Confidential Information within thirty (30) days.

7. Residual Knowledge. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, Recipient shall be free to use
for any purpose any ideas, concepts, know-how, or techniques retained in the unaided memory of Recipient's personnel
who had access to Discloser's Confidential Information, including without limitation any trade secrets of Discloser,
without restriction, accounting, or further obligation to Discloser.

8. No Warranty; No Obligation. Confidential Information is provided "as is" and nothing herein obligates either
party to proceed with any transaction.

9. Remedies. Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief for any breach of this
Agreement, without the necessity of posting bond, in addition to any other available remedies.

10. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to an affiliate or in
connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

11. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and disputes shall be resolved
in the state or federal courts located in Delaware.
