NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of August 12, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Helios
Robotics, Inc. ("Discloser") and Vantage Contract Manufacturing Ltd. ("Recipient") in connection with a possible
manufacturing subcontract (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any technical, business, or financial
information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, whether oral, written, or electronic, and whether or not marked
confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

2. Exclusions. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of Recipient, was
already known to Recipient, is independently developed without reference to Discloser's information, or is
rightfully received from a third party.

3. Use and Standard of Care. Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose. Recipient shall
be strictly liable for any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Confidential Information, regardless of the degree
of care exercised or the presence or absence of fault, and regardless of whether such use or disclosure was
intentional, negligent, or accidental.

4. Compelled Disclosure. Recipient required by law to disclose Confidential Information shall give prompt notice
before disclosure, where legally permitted, and cooperate with Discloser's efforts to seek protective treatment.

5. Term and Confidentiality Period. This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date, and
confidentiality obligations survive for four (4) years thereafter, with trade secrets protected for as long as they
remain trade secrets.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon termination or Discloser's written request, Recipient shall return or destroy all
Confidential Information, provided that copies retained solely for backup or legal-hold purposes remain subject to
this Agreement.

7. No Warranty; No Obligation. Confidential Information is provided "as is" and nothing herein obligates either
party to proceed with any transaction.

8. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to an affiliate or in
connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

9. Remedies. In the event of any actual or threatened breach by Recipient, Discloser shall be entitled to immediate
injunctive relief without any requirement to prove irreparable harm, post bond, or provide notice, and Recipient
shall indemnify and hold harmless Discloser for all losses, costs, and attorneys' fees arising from any breach,
without limitation, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

10. Miscellaneous. For a period of three (3) years following the Effective Date, Recipient agrees not to develop,
market, or sell any product or service that competes with Discloser's business as conducted on the Effective Date.
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.

11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction selected
by Discloser in its sole discretion at the time any dispute arises, and any dispute shall be resolved exclusively in
courts selected by Discloser; Recipient waives any objection to venue or jurisdiction.
