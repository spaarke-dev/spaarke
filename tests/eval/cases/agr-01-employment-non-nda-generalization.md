EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "Effective
Date") between Meridian Analytics Inc. (the "Company") and Jordan Avery ("Employee").

1. Position and Duties. The Company employs Employee as Senior Data Engineer. Employee shall perform
the duties customarily associated with that position and such other duties as the Company reasonably
assigns.

2. Compensation. The Company shall pay Employee an annual base salary of $145,000, payable in
accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices, less applicable withholdings.

3. At-Will Employment. Employment under this Agreement is at will. Either party may terminate the
employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice.

4. Confidentiality. Employee shall not, during or after employment, disclose the Company's
confidential or proprietary information to any third party except as required to perform Employee's
duties.

5. Assignment of Inventions. Employee assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in any
inventions, works of authorship, and other intellectual property that Employee conceives or reduces
to practice at any time during the term of employment, whether or not related to the Company's
business and whether or not developed using the Company's time, equipment, or facilities.

6. Non-Competition. For a period of three (3) years following the termination of employment for any
reason, Employee shall not, anywhere in the world, directly or indirectly engage in, own, manage,
be employed by, or provide services to any business that competes in any respect with the Company or
any of its affiliates.

7. Non-Solicitation. For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, Employee shall not
solicit any employee of the Company to leave the Company's employ.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to
its conflict-of-laws principles.

9. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning
Employee's employment and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
