NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of July 6, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Helios Robotics,
Inc. (the "Disclosing Party") and Summit Vendor Solutions Inc. ("Recipient") in connection with a possible vendor
qualification review (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any technical, business, or financial
information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to Recipient, whether oral, written, or electronic, and whether or
not marked confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

2. Exclusions. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of Recipient, was
already known to the Discloser prior to disclosure, is independently developed by Recipient without reference to
the Disclosing Party's information, or is rightfully received by Recipient from a third party.

3. Use and Standard of Care. Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and protect it
with reasonable care, limiting disclosure to employees with a need to know who are bound by obligations no less
protective than those herein, with Recipient remaining responsible for its personnel's compliance.

4. Compelled Disclosure. Recipient required by law to disclose Confidential Information shall give the Discloser
prompt notice before disclosure, where legally permitted, and cooperate with efforts to seek protective treatment.

5. Term and Confidentiality Period. This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date, and
confidentiality obligations survive for four (4) years thereafter, as set forth in Section 9 above, with trade
secrets protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon termination or the Disclosing Party's written request, Recipient shall return or
destroy all Confidential Information in its possession within ten (10) business days.

7. No Warranty; No Obligation. Confidential Information is provided "as is" and nothing herein obligates either
party to proceed with any transaction.

8. Remedies. The Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief for any breach of this
Agreement, without the necessity of posting bond, in addition to any other available remedies.
