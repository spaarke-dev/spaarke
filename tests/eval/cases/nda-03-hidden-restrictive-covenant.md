MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") between
Helios Robotics, Inc. ("Helios") and Cascade Field Services, Inc. ("Cascade") in connection with a possible field
-service partnership (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any technical, business, or financial
information disclosed by either party to the other, whether oral, written, or electronic, that a reasonable person
would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

2. Exclusions. Confidential Information excludes information that is public through no fault of the receiving
party, was already known to the receiving party, is independently developed without reference to the disclosing
party's information, or is rightfully received from a third party.

3. Use and Standard of Care. Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and
protect it with the same care it uses for its own information of similar importance, but no less than reasonable
care, and shall limit disclosure to employees and advisors with a need to know who are bound by obligations no less
protective than those herein, with each party remaining responsible for its recipients' compliance.

4. Compelled Disclosure. A party required by law to disclose Confidential Information shall give prompt notice
before disclosure, where legally permitted, and cooperate with efforts to seek protective treatment.

5. Term and Confidentiality Period. This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date, and
confidentiality obligations survive for four (4) years thereafter, with trade secrets protected for as long as they
remain trade secrets.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon termination or written request, the receiving party shall return or destroy all
Confidential Information, provided that copies retained solely for backup, archival, or legal-hold purposes remain
subject to this Agreement.

7. No Warranty; No Obligation. Confidential Information is provided "as is" and nothing herein obligates either
party to proceed with any transaction.

8. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to an affiliate or in
connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and disputes shall be resolved
in the state or federal courts located in Delaware.

10. Miscellaneous. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.
During the Term and for two (2) years thereafter, Recipient shall not solicit for employment or engage as a
contractor or consultant any employee, officer, or consultant of Discloser, nor shall Recipient solicit or induce
any customer or supplier of Discloser to alter its relationship with Discloser. This Agreement may be amended only
by a writing signed by both parties, and the invalidity of any provision shall not affect the remaining provisions.

11. Remedies. Each party shall have all remedies available to it at law or in equity for any breach of this
Agreement by the other party.
