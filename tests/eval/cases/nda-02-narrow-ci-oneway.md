NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of April 10, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Helios Robotics,
Inc. ("Discloser") and Northgate Prototyping Corp. ("Recipient"), in connection with Discloser's disclosure of
certain information to Recipient for the purpose of evaluating a prototyping engagement (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by Discloser
to Recipient that is marked "Confidential" in writing at the time of disclosure, or, if disclosed orally, is
summarized in a writing marked "Confidential" delivered to Recipient within ten (10) days of the oral disclosure.

2. Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available
through no fault of Recipient; (b) was rightfully known to Recipient prior to disclosure; (c) is independently
developed by Recipient without use of Discloser's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a
third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

3. Use and Standard of Care. Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and shall protect
it using a reasonable degree of care, and shall not disclose it to any third party except to employees with a need
to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those herein.

4. Compelled Disclosure. If Recipient is required by law to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall
promptly notify Discloser before disclosure, to the extent legally permitted, and shall cooperate with Discloser's
efforts to seek protective treatment.

5. Term and Confidentiality Period. This Agreement shall remain in effect for one (1) year from the Effective Date,
and Recipient's confidentiality obligations shall survive for three (3) years following termination or expiration,
provided that trade secrets remain protected for so long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon termination or Discloser's written request, Recipient shall return or destroy all
Confidential Information, provided that Recipient may retain copies required by law or made pursuant to routine
backup or archival processes, which remain subject to this Agreement.

7. No Warranty; No Obligation. All Confidential Information is provided "as is" without warranty, and nothing herein
obligates either party to proceed with any transaction.

8. Remedies. Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief for any breach of this
Agreement, without the necessity of posting bond, in addition to any other available remedies.

9. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that
either party may assign it to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

10. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute shall be resolved
in the state or federal courts located in Delaware.
