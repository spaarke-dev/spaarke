RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made as of January 15, 2026 between Fairview Properties LLC
("Landlord") and Dana Whitfield ("Tenant") for the property located at 214 Birchwood Lane, Unit 3B, Springfield,
IL (the "Premises").

1. Term. The Lease term begins February 1, 2026 and ends January 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated as provided
herein.

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of $1,850.00, due on the first day of each month. A late fee of
$75.00 applies to any payment received after the fifth day of the month.

3. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850.00 prior to occupancy, refundable within thirty
(30) days of move-out less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

4. Use of Premises. The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for Tenant and Tenant's immediate
family. No subletting is permitted without Landlord's prior written consent.

5. Utilities. Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, internet, and water/sewer charges. Landlord is
responsible for trash collection and common-area maintenance.

6. Maintenance. Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good condition and promptly notify Landlord of any needed
repairs. Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and major system failures (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)
not caused by Tenant's negligence.

7. Pets. No pets are permitted without Landlord's prior written consent and a separate pet addendum.

8. Termination. Either party may terminate this Lease at the end of the term by providing sixty (60) days' written
notice. Early termination by Tenant requires payment of an early-termination fee equal to two months' rent.

9. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first written above.
