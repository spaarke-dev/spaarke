## 2026-10-29: Seed content for Cowork practice packs

**Question**: What can claude-for-legal, mike-workflows and the Lavern vault contribute to five Cowork practice packs (NDA, CRE lease, employment, US patent applications, invoice/OCG review)? What are the licence terms, and can the material be imported to MS Cowork as-is?

**Findings**:
- **claude-for-legal** (anthropics, Apache-2.0, no NOTICE file, CLA for contributors, HEAD 4a6c651 2026-07-23): 13 plugins and 151 SKILL.md files. Skills are process-heavy and deliberately carry no default positions: "does not ship with default positions on NDA terms". Positions come from a 10–20 min `cold-start-interview` that writes `~/.claude/plugins/config/claude-for-legal/<plugin>/CLAUDE.md`. That path is referenced 939 times across 145 SKILL.md files. MS `atk import openplugin` copies skills verbatim and turns `.mcp.json` into connectors, but drops commands/, agents/ and hooks/ ("Not yet supported"). The plugin CLAUDE.md is not mapped, so **imported as-is, the skills break on the profile path**. Hooks are empty `{}`. The `.mcp.json` connectors are third-party HTTP MCPs that need customer subscriptions plus an OAuth referenceId (Cowork does not support API keys).
- **mike-workflows** (Open-Legal-Products, MIT "Copyright (c) 2026 Mike", HEAD 20bed20 2026-10-20): 179 SKILL.md files. Each is a lean (~600–2,500 words) table-output review with a party-specific checklist matrix; `jurisdictions: "General"`. Relevant: `assistant-workflows/{nda-review,commercial-lease-review,employment-agreement-review,msa-review,dpa-review,verity-the-hallucination-audit}` and `tabular-review-workflows/{nda,commercial-lease,employment-agreement}-tabular-review` (with `table-columns.yaml`). The lease content is **UK-flavoured** (upward-only rent review, service charge, underletting, break conditions). Per-skill authors vary (Aku Nikkola 80 = Finnish pack, Klotzkette, MateMatic, Victor Wang), all MIT. **mikegc-workflows** (Apache-2.0 with NOTICE) has 8 in-house skills (contract-intake, contract-markup, obligation-tracking).
- **Lavern vault** (Apache-2.0 plus NOTICE, © Antti Innanen, v0.15.0): mostly app code. The reusable content is thin: 6 templates in `src/templates/docs` (nda-mutual, nda-one-way, ...), generic specialist prompts (~1k words each, embedded in TS with debate-board tool calls), `src/knowledge/*.ts` heuristics (3.5k words), and `evals/jv`, which holds 10 CUAD JV contracts (CC BY 4.0) plus a pre-registered RUBRIC.md, self-graded. The eval *method* is reusable; the JV content is off-topic.
- **No source covers invoice/OCG billing review, US patent application drafting/review, or a US CRE lease checklist** (Lavern's real-estate-counsel prompt only lists CAM/SNDA/estoppel). Employment in claude-for-legal is US in-house (hiring/termination/classification, FMLA/WARN/OWBPA flags), but it is told not to rely on memory for state law.

**Sources**: github.com/anthropics/claude-for-legal (LICENSE, README.md, commercial-legal/skills/nda-review/SKILL.md, employment-legal/CLAUDE.md); github.com/Open-Legal-Products/mike-workflows (LICENSE, PROVENANCE.md, README.md); github.com/Open-Legal-Products/mikegc-workflows (NOTICE); C:\code_files\lavern-reference-vault (NOTICE, evals/jv/RUBRIC.md, src/templates/docs); learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development (upd 2026-10-21, "What's not converted"); cowork-plugins (upd 2026-10-29).
