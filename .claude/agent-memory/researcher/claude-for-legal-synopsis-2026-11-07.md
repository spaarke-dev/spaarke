## 2026-11-07: Claude for Legal - what it is, configuration, extension, surfaces
**Question**: For Spaarke r2 (Claude edition of the Legal AI Accelerator, "email a zip" v1): what claude-for-legal provides, how practice positions are configured, how to extend, surface support, licence.
**Findings**: `main` is still `4a6c651` (2026-07-22); about 25 open PRs, mostly connectors. 151 SKILL.md, 10 agents (frontmatter name/description/model:sonnet/tools, no schedule key), 0 commands; every hooks.json is `{"hooks":{}}`; the plugin CLAUDE.md is a template that is never loaded. The profile is prose MD written by cold-start-interview to `~/.claude/plugins/config/claude-for-legal/<plugin>/CLAUDE.md` plus a shared `company-profile.md`; skills hard-stop on `[PLACEHOLDER]`. Open issues #55/#94: Cowork can't write or keep this path, and there's no shared team profile. nda-review is method-only and refuses GREEN without attorney-reviewed `NDA triage positions`, a section the template omits (PR #125). Surfaces (claude.com/docs/plugins/platform-support): skills, commands and remote MCP work in chat, Cowork and Code; agents and hooks are ignored in chat; a top-level `bin/` can't be installed in chat or Cowork; `${user_config}` is not prompted in Cowork; M365 add-ins get skills enabled in Claude settings. Claude Code `plugin.json` supports `dependencies` (cross-marketplace needs `allowCrossMarketplaceDependenciesOn`); names starting `claude-` are reserved. Apache-2.0 with no NOTICE file. The separate Feb-2026 `legal` v1.3.0 (9 skills) lives in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins.
