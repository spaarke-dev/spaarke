# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 2 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Ostrander Biologics, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 7 Fennel Wharf, Calder Heights, Massachusetts ("Ostrander"), and **Pelham & Wick Diagnostics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 330 Orchard Lane, Stonebridge, Connecticut ("Pelham"). Each may be a "Discloser" or a "Recipient".

**1. Purpose.** The parties will discuss a possible co-development of a point-of-care assay (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means non-public scientific, technical, clinical, business and financial information disclosed by or for a Discloser in connection with the Purpose that is marked confidential, or that is identified as confidential when disclosed orally and confirmed in writing within thirty (30) days.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no breach by the Recipient; (b) the Recipient already lawfully possessed without confidentiality duty, as shown by its written records; (c) the Recipient develops independently; or (d) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and protect it with reasonable care. It may share it only with employees and advisers who need to know it and who owe it a duty of confidence.

**5. Term.** This Agreement lasts two (2) years from the Effective Date. The Recipient's confidentiality obligations continue for two (2) years after the date of each disclosure.

**6. Residuals.** The Recipient's personnel may use, in their general work, general skills, know-how and ideas of a non-specific nature retained in unaided memory. This clause does not apply to, and gives no right to use or disclose, (a) any trade secret, (b) any information on the identity of a compound, sequence, antibody or assay design, or (c) any information protected by a patent or patent application. It grants no licence under any patent or copyright.

**7. Non-Solicitation.** For eighteen (18) months after the Effective Date, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other who worked on the Purpose. General job advertisements and hiring a person who responds to them are not a breach.

**8. Remedies.** A breach may cause irreparable harm, and the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

**9. Indemnity.** The Recipient shall indemnify the Discloser against third-party claims and losses arising from the Recipient's breach of this Agreement. The Recipient's total liability under this clause is capped at US$250,000.

**10. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The state and federal courts located in Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

**11. General.** This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject. Assignment requires the other party's written consent, except to a successor to the whole of a party's business.
