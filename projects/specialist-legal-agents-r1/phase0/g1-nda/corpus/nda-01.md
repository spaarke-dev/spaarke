# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 6 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Quillfeather Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 40 Ledger Row, Marrowdale, Oregon ("Quillfeather"), and **Tidewater Lantern Co.**, a Texas limited liability company with offices at 8 Cormorant Street, Port Alder, Texas ("Tidewater"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other for the Purpose.

**1. Purpose.** The "Purpose" is evaluating a joint pilot that connects Quillfeather's warehouse sensor platform to Tidewater's dockside scheduling software.

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means non-public business, technical and financial information that one party (the "Discloser") discloses to the other (the "Recipient") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient shows (a) is or becomes public through no act of the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient, without restriction, before disclosure; (c) was independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) was lawfully received from a third party entitled to disclose it.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own information of like kind, and no less than reasonable care. The Recipient may share it only with its employees and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties no less protective than this Agreement.

**5. Compelled Disclosure.** If the law requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient shall, where lawful, give the Discloser prompt written notice and disclose only what is required.

**6. Term.** This Agreement lasts one (1) year from the Effective Date. The Recipient's obligations under this Agreement end when this Agreement expires.

**7. Residuals.** Neither party is restricted from using ideas, concepts, know-how and techniques of a general nature that remain in the unaided memory of its personnel after they have had access to Confidential Information, including in the development of its own products and services.

**8. Return or Destruction.** Within thirty (30) days after the Discloser's written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information and confirm that it has done so.

**9. Remedies.** Each party acknowledges that unauthorised use or disclosure may cause harm for which damages are inadequate, and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to its other remedies.

**10. Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. The state and federal courts located in Bexar County, Texas have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

**11. General.** This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject. Neither party may assign it without the other's written consent. It may be signed in counterparts.
