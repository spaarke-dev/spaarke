# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (ONE-WAY)

This Confidentiality Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 19 January 2026 between **Tamarack Ridge Foods LLC**, a Florida limited liability company with offices at 215 Saltmeadow Avenue, Greywater Bay, Florida (the "Discloser"), and **Northgate Lumenworks Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 90 Anvil Court, Kestrel Falls, Ohio (the "Recipient").

**1. Purpose.** The Discloser will share information about its recipes, supplier terms and plant design so that the Recipient can assess whether to bid to supply packaging machinery (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information the Discloser or its representatives disclose to the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, whether or not marked, including information disclosed orally or by site visit, together with notes and analyses that contain or reflect it.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient proves by written records (a) is public other than through the Recipient's breach; (b) was lawfully in its possession, free of any confidentiality duty, before disclosure; (c) was independently developed without reference to Confidential Information; or (d) was lawfully received from a third party without breach of any duty to the Discloser.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, shall not reverse engineer any sample or equipment provided, and shall protect it with at least reasonable care. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its employees, advisers and contractors to whom it gives access.

**5. Term.** The Recipient's obligations apply to Confidential Information for five (5) years from the date it is disclosed. Information that qualifies as a trade secret stays protected for as long as it remains a trade secret under applicable law.

**6. Non-Solicitation.** For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, the Recipient shall not solicit for employment any employee of the Discloser with whom it had contact through the Purpose. General advertising not aimed at the Discloser's employees is not a solicitation.

**7. Compelled Disclosure.** If the law requires disclosure, the Recipient shall give the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful, disclose only the part that its counsel advises in writing is legally required, and cooperate with the Discloser's efforts to protect it.

**8. Return or Destruction.** Within ten (10) days after the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify in writing that it has done so.

**9. Remedies.** The Recipient acknowledges that a breach would cause irreparable harm. The Discloser may obtain injunctive relief without posting a bond and without proving actual damages.

**11. General.** Nothing in this Agreement limits any right or remedy of the Discloser under trade-secret law. The Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the Discloser's written consent.
