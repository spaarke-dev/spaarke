# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 8 April 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Lumen Harrow Marine LLC**, a Florida limited liability company with offices at 33 Pilot Boat Road, Coral Anchorage, Florida ("Lumen Harrow"), and **Ashgrove Tidal Power Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 410 Breakwater Drive, Selkirk Sound, Washington ("Ashgrove").

**1. Purpose.** The parties will explore a possible joint bid for a harbour-side tidal energy installation (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means non-public technical, commercial and financial information disclosed by a party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient") for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked confidential or that the Recipient knows or should reasonably know is confidential.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient shows (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) it lawfully knew before disclosure, free of restriction; (c) it independently developed; or (d) it lawfully received from a third party free of restriction.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and protect it with reasonable care. It may share it only with representatives who need to know it and who are bound by written or professional duties of confidence.

**5. Term.** The Recipient's obligations apply for five (5) years from the Effective Date. The Recipient shall continue to protect any Confidential Information that is a trade secret for as long as it remains a trade secret.

**6. Residuals.** The Recipient's personnel may use general know-how and skills retained in unaided memory. This clause does not allow use or disclosure of the Discloser's trade secrets, patented or patent-pending inventions, or source code, and it grants no licence under any intellectual property right.

**7. Compelled Disclosure.** If the law requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, it shall give the Discloser prompt written notice where lawful and disclose only the portion that is legally required.

**8. Return or Destruction.** Within thirty (30) days after the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information.

**9. Indemnity.** The Recipient shall indemnify the Discloser against direct losses arising from the Recipient's breach of this Agreement. The Recipient's total liability under this Agreement, including under this clause, shall not exceed US$1,000,000.

**10. Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. The state and federal courts located in Miami-Dade County, Florida have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

**11. General.** This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject. Neither party may assign it without the other's written consent.
