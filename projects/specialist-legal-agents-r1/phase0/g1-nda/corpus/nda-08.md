# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (ONE-WAY)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 22 April 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Fernhollow Games, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with offices at 2 Lantern Mews, Brackenridge, Georgia (the "Discloser"), and **Saltmarsh Interactive LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 118 Reed Bank Road, Wickford Green, Colorado (the "Recipient").

**1. Purpose.** The Discloser will disclose unreleased game design documents, engine tools and player-retention data so that the Recipient can evaluate a co-publishing deal (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information that the Discloser discloses to the Recipient for the Purpose, in any form, whether or not marked, including builds, design documents and analytics.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was in the Recipient's possession before disclosure, as shown by written records; (c) the Recipient develops independently without reference to Confidential Information; or (d) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party who owes no duty to the Discloser.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, shall protect it with at least reasonable care, and shall not decompile or reverse engineer any build or tool. It may disclose Confidential Information only to employees who need to know it and are bound in writing.

**5. Term.** The Recipient's obligations last three (3) years from the Effective Date. Confidential Information that is a trade secret remains protected for as long as it qualifies as a trade secret.

**6. Residuals.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Recipient from using ideas, concepts, mechanics, know-how and techniques that its personnel retain in unaided memory after exposure to Confidential Information, in any of the Recipient's products.

**7. Non-Solicitation.** For eighteen (18) months after the Effective Date, the Recipient shall not solicit for employment any employee or contractor of the Discloser who worked on the Purpose.

**8. Remedies and Indemnity.** The Recipient acknowledges that a breach would cause irreparable harm and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief. The Recipient shall also indemnify and hold harmless the Discloser from all losses, liabilities, damages and expenses, including legal fees, arising from any breach or alleged breach of this Agreement. This indemnity applies without any monetary limit.

**9. Return or Destruction.** Within twenty (20) days after the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information.

**10. Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. The state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

**11. General.** This is the parties' entire agreement on its subject. The Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the Discloser's written consent.
