# MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 11 May 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Dovecote Mercantile, Inc.**, a New York corporation with offices at 52 Chandler Walk, Ravensmere, New York ("Dovecote"), and **Kingfisher Row Textiles LLC**, a North Carolina limited liability company with offices at 14 Spindle Street, Alderton, North Carolina ("Kingfisher").

**1. Purpose.** The parties will discuss a possible private-label supply arrangement for home textiles (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means non-public business, pricing, design and supplier information that a party (the "Discloser") discloses to the other (the "Recipient") for the Purpose and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient; (b) the Recipient already lawfully held free of restriction; (c) the Recipient independently develops; or (d) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party free of restriction.

**4. Use and Protection.** The Recipient shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, shall not use it to develop products or sourcing arrangements for itself, and shall protect it with reasonable care. It may share it only with employees and advisers who need to know it and are bound by duties of confidence.

**5. Term.** The Recipient's obligations apply for two (2) years from the Effective Date.

**6. Non-Solicitation.** For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other with whom it dealt in connection with the Purpose, other than through general advertising.

**7. Compelled Disclosure.** If the law requires disclosure of Confidential Information, the Recipient shall give prompt written notice where lawful and disclose only what is required.

**8. Return or Destruction.** Within thirty (30) days after the Discloser's request, the Recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information.

**9. Remedies and Indemnity.** A breach may cause irreparable harm, and the Discloser may seek injunctive relief. The Recipient shall indemnify the Discloser for losses caused by the Recipient's breach, up to a maximum total of US$500,000.

**10. Governing Law and Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising under it.

**11. General.** This is the parties' entire agreement on its subject. Neither party may assign it without the other's written consent.
