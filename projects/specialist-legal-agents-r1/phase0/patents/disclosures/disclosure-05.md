# Test disclosure 05: a bagless vacuum cleaner with two cyclones, one inside the other

> Test content for Phase 0 (spec FR-09). This is a short invention summary written for testing from the public record of the patent named above. It is not the inventor's text and does not reproduce the patent's claims. Draft from this disclosure only; do not look up or recall the published claims.

## Problem

A bag or filter vacuum cleaner loses suction as the bag or filter clogs with fine dust, and the bags must be bought and replaced. A single cyclone separator can spin dirt out of the air without a bag, but one cyclone cannot handle both kinds of dirt well: a cyclone efficient enough to catch fine dust has a narrow, fast flow that large debris and fluff block, and a wide cyclone that tolerates fluff lets fine dust through.

## The idea

Use two cyclones in series, each sized for one job, and nest the second inside the first so the appliance stays compact:
- **Outer cyclone (low efficiency):** a wide chamber, roughly cylindrical. Dirty air from the cleaner head enters it tangentially, so it swirls around the wall. Heavy debris and fluff are thrown out of the airflow and fall to the bottom of the chamber.
- **Inner cyclone (high efficiency):** a narrower, frusto-conical chamber whose lower end is smaller than its upper end, mounted inside the outer chamber and concentric with it. Air that has passed the outer stage is led through a passage to an entry port near the top of the inner cyclone, again tangentially. The faster, tighter swirl separates the fine dust, which collects at the narrow lower end.
- **Air path:** the cleaned air leaves through the upper end of the inner cyclone into a passage that leads through a motor-driven fan to the exhaust; the fan draws the air through the whole appliance.
- **Emptying:** the cyclone casings can be lifted off and separated from one another, so both collected loads are tipped out. No bag or filter needs replacing.

## Embodiments described

- A portable cleaner, in which the suction pipe also serves as a handle or connects to other suction tools.
- An upright cleaner with wheels and a handle, carrying the cyclone unit.
- Coarse dirt settles in the lower part of the outer cyclone and fine dust in the inner cyclone, so the two are collected in separate bins.

## What the inventor considers new

A vacuum cleaning appliance that separates dirt in two cyclone stages in series, a lower-efficiency outer cyclone for coarse debris and a higher-efficiency inner cyclone nested coaxially within it for fine dust, with no bag or filter needed, and parts that separate for emptying.
