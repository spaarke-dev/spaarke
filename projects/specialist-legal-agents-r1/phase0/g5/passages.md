## P1: mutual NDA, three-year term

> This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is made between Harrowgate Components Ltd and Vellum Quarry Software Inc. 1. Each party may disclose confidential information to the other to evaluate a possible supply relationship. 2. The recipient will use the information only for that purpose and protect it with reasonable care. 3. Confidential information excludes information that is public, already known to the recipient, independently developed, or received from a third party without a duty of confidence. 4. The obligations continue for three years from the date of disclosure. 5. On request the recipient will return or destroy the information. 6. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## P2: NDA clauses, broad definition, long survival, non-solicit, distant venue

> "Confidential Information" means all information of any kind, in any form, whether or not marked, that the Discloser or its affiliates have ever shared with the Recipient or that the Recipient could reasonably infer is valuable. The Recipient's duties survive for seven years after the end of the discussions. During that period and for twelve months afterwards, the Recipient will not solicit for employment any employee of the Discloser. Any dispute will be heard exclusively in the courts of Port Verrin, Republic of Calderon, whose law will apply, and the Recipient waives any objection to that venue. Orlund Freight Partners is the Discloser and Tessaly Bay Analytics is the Recipient.

## P3: one-way NDA with a residuals clause and no return-or-destroy duty

> This one-way Confidentiality Agreement is between Pennyfold Robotics Inc. ("Discloser") and Marrow Lantern Foods Ltd ("Recipient"). The Recipient will keep the Discloser's technical and commercial information confidential and use it only to assess a pilot project, for four years from signing. Nothing in this agreement restricts the Recipient from using ideas, concepts and know-how that its personnel retain in memory after reviewing the information ("Residuals"), and the Recipient may use Residuals for any purpose. The Recipient has no obligation to return or destroy any materials. The agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

## P4: services agreement, uncapped liability, net-90 payment, broad vendor assignment, data protection

> Services Agreement between Kestrel Hollow Retail Inc. (Customer) and Ambrose Tide Systems Ltd (Vendor). Vendor will provide the platform support described in Schedule A. Customer will pay each invoice within ninety days of receipt. Vendor's liability under this agreement is unlimited. Vendor may assign this agreement, or any right under it, to any person without Customer's consent. Vendor will process Customer personal data only on Customer's written instructions, apply appropriate security measures, tell Customer of a data breach within seventy-two hours, and delete the data when the services end. The term is two years.

## P5: licence and supply agreement, one-sided indemnity, auto-renewal, warranty disclaimer, mixed governing law

> Licence and Supply Agreement between Brindlewood Instruments Ltd (Supplier) and Quenby Ridge Laboratories Inc. (Buyer). Buyer will indemnify Supplier against all claims arising from its use of the products; Supplier gives Buyer no indemnity. This agreement renews for successive one-year terms unless Buyer gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and twenty days before the term ends. Supplier disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including merchantability and fitness for purpose. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, except that disputes about the licence are governed by the laws of Ireland and heard in Dublin.
