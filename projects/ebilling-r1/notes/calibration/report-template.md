## 1. Summary

- **On its own, the analyzer's confidence barely tells right values from wrong ones.** On the GA analyzer, accuracy is between 98.2% and 99.3% in every bin from 0.50 up, so a higher score doesn't mean a more accurate value. The scores are also too low: values the analyzer scores about 0.8 are right about 99% of the time. The expected calibration error is 0.19 (GA) and 0.25 (preview).
- **At the seeded threshold of 0.90, analyzer confidence alone trusts some wrong values and flags most values for review.**
  - GA: 805 values trusted, 11 of them wrong (1.4%); 91.8% of values flagged.
  - Preview: 1,056 trusted, 6 wrong (0.6%); 89.3% flagged.
- **No analyzer-only threshold meets NFR-06 (zero misread-caused rejections) at a usable review load.**
  - GA still trusts 2 wrong values at 0.99.
  - Preview reaches zero only at 0.99, where it flags 99.4% of values.
- **The other D-47 signals are what can close the gap.**
  - 15 of the 17 trusted-but-wrong values, across both analyzers, are fields that a reference-data check (12) or the arithmetic check (3) covers.
  - The arithmetic upper bound lifts fee hours, rate and amount from about 2% to 11–17% reach at 0.90, with no wrong value trusted.
  - The remaining gap is **dates read day-for-month**: two GA invoice dates at 0.992, on non-US invoices. No D-47 signal covers them (section 7).
- **Recommendation:** keep the threshold, weights and caps as seeded (section 8). Add a date-ambiguity check before go-live (filed for 162). Re-decide once the owner-approved dev run gives the per-signal breakdown.

## 2. Method

- **Data:** 005's recorded responses, re-scored per field against the ground truth from 002. The two analyzers are scored separately, never pooled (D-81):
  - **GA:** `sprk_invoice_v1`, API 2025-11-01;
  - **preview:** `sprk_invoice_v1p`, API 2026-06-01-preview.
- **Matching:** the same as 005's `score.py`: the header values the analyzer returned, and the fields of matched lines as returned (the "printed" view). Spurious rows count as wrong on items whose ground truth is complete. New here: results are kept per field, and the fee task and activity codes as billed are scored.
- **Bins:** 0–0.50, 0.50–0.70, 0.70–0.80, 0.80–0.90, 0.90–0.95, 0.95–1.00.
- **Wrong rate at the threshold:** the share of values at or above 0.90 that are wrong. These are the misreads the gate would trust.
- **Reliability:** per bin, the mean confidence against the observed accuracy. ECE is the count-weighted absolute gap.
- **Sweep:** thresholds 0.70–0.99 in steps of 0.01. At each step it reports trusted values, trusted-and-wrong values, and the share flagged for review.
- **Combined confidence:** `calib_lib.combined()` reproduces 046's `CombinedConfidenceCalculator`, pinned to the C# test vectors. Combined scores need the signal breakdown that only the dev run records, so this baseline reports the analyzer signal plus an arithmetic upper bound (section 6).
- **Check against 005:** the baseline reproduces 005's numbers (GA: 7,512 values, 619 at 0.90 or above, 6 wrong) when code fields are excluded. That basis row is in each bin table.

| Setting | Seeded | Recommended now | Why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| sprk_reliabilitythreshold | 0.90 | **0.90 (no change)** | Analyzer confidence can't justify any other value (section 5). NFR-06 depends on the caps, not on the threshold. |

| sprk_arithmeticfailcap | 0.50 | 0.50 | Keeps every value that fails arithmetic in review |

- **Analyzer choice (D-81).** Among trusted values, preview has a lower wrong rate than GA at every threshold from 0.86 up, and no date misread. That supports keeping preview as the primary analyzer. The owner confirms after the dev run.
- **Expected effect:**
  - **NFR-06:** with the caps, every trusted misread in this baseline except the two GA dates falls below the threshold.
  - **On preview,** the remaining exposure is zero, provided the matter reference is checked against the matter (section 7).
  - **On GA,** it is the date gap in section 7.
  - **Review load:** stays high, around 90% of values flagged by analyzer confidence. The arithmetic and reference matches are what lower it. The dev run measures by how much.
- **162 applies any change.** 161 changes no setting.
