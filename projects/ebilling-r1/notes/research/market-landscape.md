## 1. Executive summary

1. **The market consolidated in 2025.** WK bought Brightflag (€425M), PERSUIT bought Apperio, Sedgwick bought Bottomline's Legal-X / Legal eXchange unit (~300 staff, P&C insurance), Onit bought Legal Files and LawBase and is folding SimpleLegal into Unity. The three largest incumbents (Thomson Reuters Legal Tracker, Wolters Kluwer ELM Solutions, LexisNexis CounselLink) plus Mitratech and Onit now own almost every enterprise seat. Sources in §4.
2. **AI invoice review moved from "flag" to "adjust" in 2026.** WK's *LegalVIEW BillAnalyzer Invoice Review Agent* (2026-05-06) "identifies non-compliant invoice line items and automatically implements adjustments" with a documented rationale, customer-set guardrails and reversibility. Onit's Spend Agent went Sentry (flag) → auto-approve clean invoices (Q1 2026) → "basic auto adjustments" (Q2 2026). LawVu auto-deducts non-compliant amounts on LEDES invoices (PDF invoices are flag-only). Details in §3.
3. **OCG-document ingestion is now real but uneven.** LawVu (upload Word/PDF guidelines + rate sheet → OCR → rule extraction), Onit (natural-language guidelines uploaded per vendor), CounselAudit (import OCG → map each clause to a check) and the firm-side tools (PointOne, Antidote) all ingest the document. Legal Tracker, CounselLink, Mitratech, Litify and SimpleLegal still describe **configured rules**, with AI applied to the invoice text rather than to the guideline document.
4. **Law firms are now running AI against the same guidelines before they submit.** Elite's May 2026 data (≈400 firms, half the Am Law 200) shows rejection rates rising 11%→18% in 2025 as client-side AI tightened; 71% of firms still manage OCG compliance manually. Start-ups PointOne ($16M Series A, Mar 2026) and Antidote ($5M seed, Jan 2026) sell pre-bill OCG compliance to firms. PERSUIT argues this leads to a "GenAI stalemate" in which post-hoc invoice review stops finding savings and control must shift upstream (rates, scoping, WIP). §3.14, §5c.
5. **Microsoft 365 integration is shallow everywhere.** Outlook add-ins are common (Legal Tracker, TeamConnect, Brightflag, CounselLink, Onit, LawVu) but mostly for filing email into matters or approving invoices from the reading pane. Only LawVu ships a Teams app and a Copilot plugin (read-only queries, invoice actions "later"). Nobody runs on Dataverse/Power Platform; no vendor exposes a Copilot agent for invoice review. Brightflag and LawVu instead expose **MCP** servers for Claude/ChatGPT. §5d.
6. **Pricing is opaque at the enterprise end and collapsing at the low end.** Enterprise vendors quote on spend-under-management or modules; firms submit free on Legal Tracker and CounselLink. CounselAudit lists $99/seat/month and claims to be ">90% less expensive than enterprise invoice review platforms"; Lawcadia lists A$500/month. §2 table B.
7. **Outcome claims are almost all vendor-authored.** The only third-party-method numbers found are two commissioned Forrester TEI studies: Brightflag (Jan 2026: 387% ROI, <6-month payback, ~5.4% risk-adjusted spend savings on a $5M composite) and Legal Tracker (2023: 372% ROI, ~6-month payback). Everything else ("up to 15%", "5–10%", "26%", "97% predictive accuracy") is marketing.

## 3. Per-vendor findings

### 3.1 Thomson Reuters Legal Tracker (+ Legal Tracker Advanced, CoCounsel)

- **Positioning / scale.** "Every AmLaw 200 firm already uses Legal Tracker"; 164 of the Fortune 500 and 343 of the Fortune 1000 (vendor claims). [Product page](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/c/streamline-and-save-with-genai-powered-spend-management-software) (fetched 2026-10-14); [TR blog](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/blog/ai-driven-legal-spend-management-with-legal-tracker-and-legal-tracker-advanced/) (2024-11-08).
- **Intake.** LEDES 98B / XML via firm portal; "convert PDF invoices to audit-ready LEDES files"; "there's no fee for firms to submit invoices." [Product page](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/c/streamline-and-save-with-genai-powered-spend-management-software). LEDES posting: "errors prevent invoices from posting, but warnings don't"; each error names the file item and segment; re-posting an existing invoice is rejected as a duplicate; client/matter numbers must match exactly. [LEDES 1998 posting errors](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/law-firm/issues-and-error-messages/ledes-1998-posting-errors.html); [LEDES XML posting errors](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/company/invoices/ledes-xml-invoice-posting-errors.html) (fetched 2026-10-14). Invoices are held with "Delivery on Hold" status until the matter profile, budget or status report is complete. [Law-firm action items](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/law-firm/action-items/action-items.html).
- **AI review (Aug 2025 "AI-enhanced Legal Tracker").** Own AI Assistant; "automatically detects billing issues, including duplicate and administrative charges"; converts invoices into standardised formats; AI dashboards with natural-language Q&A and anomaly detection. [TR press release](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2025/august/thomson-reuters-takes-major-step-to-transform-corporate-workflows-with-launch-of-cocounsel-legal-ai-enhanced-legal-tracker-and-cocounsel-tax.html) (2025-08-05). Earlier: duplicate detection, "flags excessive timekeeper hours", block-billing audit efficiency, NLP models to "catch billing violations that previously required manual review." [TR blog](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/blog/ai-driven-legal-spend-management-with-legal-tracker-and-legal-tracker-advanced/) (2024-11-08). The product page says the AI *flags* items for review and "Configure how much it does, then let it run"; it does not claim auto-adjustment.
- **Guidelines & rates.** Configured rules ("Outside Counsel Guideline alerts", matters created "with budgets and approved rates" — customer testimonials). Legal Tracker Advanced adds rate-request automation (auto-accept, auto-reject, rate freezing) and Westlaw-powered analytics on firms, courts and judges. [TR blog](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/blog/ai-driven-legal-spend-management-with-legal-tracker-and-legal-tracker-advanced/) (2024-11-08).
- **Appeals.** Firm user who posted the invoice receives an email and an Action Items alert when an invoice is reduced, rejected or disputed; no notification on full approval; no in-system appeal workflow is documented. [Notification help](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/company/invoices/notification-disputed-reduced-rejected.html). Adjustments by a higher-level reviewer are routed back to all previous reviewers. [Adjustments help](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/company/invoices/adjustments). Appeals handling is marketed via the LegalBillReview.com partnership (attorney reviewers who "manage appeals, apply invoice-level adjustments … and finalize bill approvals" inside Legal Tracker; LBR claims 12% average savings). [TR post](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/posts/innovation/legal-tracker-continues-delivering-benefits-and-results-to-legal-departments-worldwide/) (2024-11-07).
- **Accruals / budgets.** Accruals module requests unbilled amounts monthly, quarterly or at fiscal year-end; pending-budget and status-report action items for firms. [Law-firm action items](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/law-firm/action-items/action-items.html).
- **Analytics.** Benchmarks against "$310 billion of market data"; spend intelligence surfaced inside CoCounsel Legal. [Product page](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/c/streamline-and-save-with-genai-powered-spend-management-software).
- **Microsoft 365.** *Tracker for Outlook* (AppSource WA104379799): approvers act on pending invoices (first 100) from the reading pane; Quick Approve ceiling; excluded if over limit, incomplete tasks, audit exceptions or waiting on another approver; same permissions as in-app. [Reviewing pending invoices in Outlook](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/company/tracker-for-outlook/reviewing-pending-invoices-in-outlook); [AppSource](https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-us/product/office/WA104379799). - **PERSUIT BillClear** — see §3.9. **Lumay.ai / Accurate Legal Billing** publish firm-side "rejection reduction" guides with untraceable benchmarks (not relied on). [Lumay](https://www.lumay.ai/blogs/reduce-invoice-rejections-in-corporate-law-firms).
- Implication: by 2026 both sides of the invoice run AI against the same OCG text. Whoever makes the rules machine-readable *and shareable* (so firms can self-check against the exact rule set) removes the appeal loop instead of automating it.

## 5. Synthesis

### 5a. Table stakes — what every e-billing product has (and buyers will assume)

Evidence: §2 tables; features present in ≥ 4 of the 12 vendors.

1. **LEDES ingestion with validation at upload** — 98B, 98BI and XML 2.x; structural errors block posting with coded, segment-level messages (Legal Tracker), duplicate-invoice rejection, exact client/matter-number matching, LEDES Error Codes V2.
2. **PDF / scanned intake with extraction** — now universal: TR PDF→LEDES, WK Intelligent Invoice Conversion, Mitratech ARIES, LawVu (Azure Document Intelligence), Brightflag, CounselAudit. LawVu's "~90% of invoices are PDFs" shows why.
3. **Free firm portal** with timekeeper registration and **rate approval before billing** (CounselLink fee offers; Legal Tracker approved rates; Litify rate-card blocking).
4. **Configurable billing-rules engine** keyed to UTBMS task/activity/expense codes, timekeeper class, rate, units, dates, matter type; rule sets per matter type / vendor.
5. **Line-item review UI** with flag → adjust/reject/accept, reason codes, line-level commentary, invoice-level adjustments, bulk actions.
6. **Multi-level approval routing** with thresholds, re-routing when a later reviewer adjusts, delegation; approve-from-Outlook add-ins (TR, Mitratech).
7. **Firm notification** on reduce/reject/dispute (email + in-portal action item) and **an appeal loop** (Passport's pay-approved-portion + iterate is the reference design; CounselLink: line-level only).
8. **Accruals** (firm-submitted unbilled amounts on a cadence) and **budgets** (matter/phase, UTBMS phases, threshold alerts, firm acknowledgement).
9. **AP/ERP hand-off** — SAP Ariba, Coupa, NetSuite, Oracle, QuickBooks; payment-status visibility for firms.
10. **Multi-currency and tax handling** (VAT/GST/QST/WHT, withholding, multiple taxes per line, pro-forma invoices, tax-portal holds) — Mitratech, Onit, Litify, LEDES XML 2.2 tiered taxes.
11. **Dashboards and standard reports**; natural-language Q&A is now expected (TR, Mitratech, Brightflag, Legal Decoder).
12. **Audit trail, SSO/SAML, role-based access, ISO 27001 / SOC 2** (LawVu lists all; Mitratech SAML; Onit "full audit logging").

### 5b. Where AI is genuinely differentiating in 2026

1. **OCG document → executable rules with provenance.** Only LawVu, Onit (NL guidelines per vendor), CounselAudit (clause → check mapping) and the firm-side tools ingest the guideline document; the incumbents still configure rules by hand and apply AI to invoice text. Lineage (which clause produced which flag) is a visible gap in every public description.
2. **Narrative-level semantic checks** that rules cannot express: block billing, administrative/clerical tasks billed as legal work, overstaffed meetings, partner doing associate work, duplicate narratives across invoices, vague descriptions. Brightflag, CounselAudit, Legal Decoder and Legal Tracker all market these; a G2 reviewer notes Brightflag "sometimes misses … block billing," so accuracy is still a differentiator.
3. **Agentic adjustment with rationale, guardrails and reversibility.** WK (May 2026) is the first incumbent to auto-apply adjustments; Onit reached "basic auto adjustments" for rule-based variances in May 2026; LawVu auto-deducts on LEDES only. Every vendor couples this with per-vendor mode switches (Onit Sentry/Automatic) and "the department decides what the AI can and cannot change." The pattern buyers now expect: *auto-approve clean → auto-adjust clear rule breaches → human for judgment calls*, each with an exportable log.
4. **Firm-facing explanations and pre-checks.** Onit BillingPoint shows Failed with plain-language reasons and suggested fixes; PERSUIT BillClear lets firms fix flags before submission. This is where disputes are actually avoided.
5. **Invoice summaries and reviewer triage** (Brightflag Invoice Summaries, Onit AI summaries, CounselLink Protégé, Legal Decoder stoplight) — reduces per-invoice review time (Forrester: 30 → 6 minutes for Brightflag).
6. **Benchmark data moats** — TR $310B, WK/LegalVIEW >$200B, Bodhala, Brightflag rates reports. Not replicable by a new entrant; a reason to position on *rule fidelity and workflow* rather than market benchmarks in r1.
7. **AI-workspace exposure (MCP)** — Brightflag, LawVu, CounselAudit expose spend data to Claude/ChatGPT with inherited permissions. No vendor yet exposes this through Microsoft 365 Copilot.
8. **Not differentiating any more:** PDF extraction, duplicate detection, rate-vs-approved checks, NL dashboards. These are expected.

### 5c. Common pain points (customers and law firms)

In-house (user reviews and surveys):
- **Rule setup and admin burden** — "billing-rule setup is time-intensive without dedicated operations staff"; edge cases need manual exception handling (rfp.wiki aggregate; SimpleLegal/Onit "hard to set up a rule"). [rfp.wiki CounselLink vs Onit](https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/lexisnexis-counsellink/onit); [G2 SimpleLegal](https://www.g2.com/products/simplelegal-an-onit-product/reviews)
- **Reporting rigidity** — Brightflag "not many out of the box reports", Legal Tracker "report generator … cumbersome", SimpleLegal reporting "not the easiest." [G2 Brightflag](https://www.g2.com/products/brightflag/reviews); [G2 Legal Tracker](https://www.g2.com/products/thomson-reuters-legal-tracker/reviews)
- **Workflow inflexibility** — cannot skip the guideline reviewer (Legal Tracker); rigid for non-standard billing arrangements (Brightflag); cannot edit sent budget requests (SimpleLegal); comments not notified, no draft-vs-draft redline (Brightflag).
- **Visibility gaps** — holds during accrual period, remaining budget (Legal Tracker); budget approval verification (TyMetrix).
- **Cost and opacity** — "pricey", "expensive add-on", "cost for service is high"; spend-based fees "inflexible"; matter management sold separately (Brightflag); Spend Agent a paid add-on (Onit).
- **Speed and support** — CounselLink "5–10 business days to process an invoice", "site is often slow"; Legal Tracker "hard to reach customer service"; Onit chat support removed.
- **AI misses** — block billing and mislabelled communications (Brightflag reviewer).
- **Integration setup** — AP connections slower than expected (Brightflag); Duck Creek sync lag (Legal Tracker).
- **Time** — 87% of in-house respondents spend < 4 hours/month on bill review; ~60% lack formal OCGs (LegalBillReview survey, vendor-run).

Law firms:
- **Rejections rising** — 11% → 18% in 2025 as client AI tightened; 71% manage OCG compliance manually; e-billing is the top revenue-cycle challenge for 48% of Global 200 CFOs (Elite, vendor data).
- **"Invoice table-tennis"** — post-hoc rejection/short-pay cycles, ambiguous OCGs "open to interpretation", write-downs and partner time on appeals (PERSUIT; ABA Law Practice 2026).
- **Format rejections** — invoices "rejected as invalid format even when the format is correct" (TyMetrix user); LEDES posting errors from firm T&B systems that the client platform will not help with (Legal Tracker help).
- **One login per client** — firms must use "a P2 site or a client specific site" per client (CounselLink reviewer); separate portals for every client programme.
- **Fees** — some programmes historically charged firms (e.g. TyMetrix "US$2,200 a year per client", undated); TR and LexisNexis advertise free submission. [Canadian Lawyer](https://www.canadianlawyermag.com/news/features/tech-support-preparing-for-the-e-billing-revolution/267179)
- **Deadlines and partial appeals** — client programmes limit appeals to adjusted lines, up to the adjustment amount, within e.g. 45 days (CounselLink client guides).

### 5d. White space for a Dataverse / M365-native, AI-first platform

1. **No e-billing product runs on Dataverse / Power Platform.** The only Microsoft-stack legal products found are firm practice-management (Curo365, sa.global, Legal One Xtra on Business Central) or consultancy builds (HSO). A Dataverse-native eBilling module inherits tenant identity, row-level security, Purview, Power BI and Copilot surfaces that every competitor must bolt on. [AppSource Legal One Xtra](https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-us/product/web-apps/PUBID.o2tiapplicationsnv1714656849391|AID.legal_one_xtra|PAPPID.2494bb5e-9cf3-4ed2-b825-5d567b87d0f6); [Curo365 profile](https://www.legaltechnologyhub.com/vendors/curo366-by-curo365); [HSO](https://www.hso.com/consultancy-offering/power-platform-for-legal/)
2. **Email as a first-class intake channel.** Competitors treat email as filing (Outlook add-ins) or a side door (Brightflag "from email", CounselAudit email upload). A monitored AP mailbox plus Outlook "forward-to-save" that turns a PDF into an invoice *document* in SPE and a Dataverse record — with the firm, matter and OCG resolved automatically — is not offered by any incumbent.
3. **Teams / Copilot as the review surface.** Only LawVu has a Teams app and a read-only Copilot plugin; nobody approves or adjusts invoices from Teams or Copilot. Approve/adjust/dispute from an adaptive card, and a Copilot agent that answers "why was line 14 cut?" from the Decision Record, is open.
4. **Guideline provenance.** LawVu and Onit ingest the OCG but expose only the resulting flag. Spaarke can show clause → rule → flag → adjustment → appeal as one traceable chain, with the OCG itself stored as a versioned SPE document next to the invoice. This also answers the "ambiguous guideline" complaint on both sides.
5. **Shared rules for firms (end the stalemate).** Publishing the client's machine-readable rule set to the portal (pre-submission check with the same engine, like BillClear/BillingPoint) converts immediate rejection from a punishment into a service; firm-side tools (PointOne, Antidote) are building the mirror image.
6. **Mid-market pricing and packaging.** Enterprise tools are quote-based on spend under management with paid AI add-ons; CounselAudit's $99/seat shows the floor. A Dataverse app priced per user/ per module, with AI review included, lands between them.
7. **Switching windows created by consolidation.** WK must reconcile Brightflag with TyMetrix/Passport/BillAnalyzer; Onit is migrating SimpleLegal customers to Unity; PERSUIT absorbed Apperio. Overlapping roadmaps and forced migrations historically open RFPs.
8. **Document-centric invoices.** Treating the invoice (and the OCG, rate card, engagement letter, budget) as documents in the same SPE container as the matter — searchable, citable, retention-managed — is unique to a platform whose core is already document + record. Competitors store the invoice as a record with an attachment.
9. **Where not to compete in r1.** Benchmark datasets ($310B / $200B), managed-review services, insurance-claims verticals (Passport, Legal-X, Litify), RFP/rate-negotiation suites (PERSUIT, Unity RFP, LegalCollaborator). Partner or defer.

### 5e. First-release vs later features (as competitors actually sequenced them)

| Typically in a first release | Typically added later (evidence) |
|---|---|
| LEDES 98B/98BI (+ XML) upload with structural validation and error codes | XML 2.2 tiered taxes, multi-currency/VAT/GST/WHT, pro-forma invoices (Onit added pro-forma Aug 2026; Mitratech international capture Dec 2025) |
| PDF intake with AI extraction to header + lines (LawVu shipped extraction Nov 2024, guideline AI a year later) | Historical PDF back-fill, non-LEDES international formats |
| Firm portal (free), timekeeper registration, rate approval gating submission | RFP / reverse-auction rate negotiation (Onit Unity RFP May 2026), rate benchmarking, auto-accept/reject rate requests (Legal Tracker Advanced) |
| Configured billing rules + UTBMS; AI line flags with line commentary; immediate rejection on hard rules (rates, timekeepers, duplicates, format) | OCG-document ingestion to rules (LawVu 2025, beta → GA), semantic narrative checks, auto-adjust (Onit: Sentry Aug 2025 → auto-approve Q1 2026 → basic auto-adjust Q2 2026 → adjustment review UX Sept 2026; WK agent May 2026) |
| Reviewer UI: approve / reduce / reject per line and invoice; multi-level approval routing; firm email + action-item notifications | Formal appeals module with partial payment and iterative negotiation (Passport sells it as a module; Onit documents it separately), managed review services |
| AP/ERP export and payment-status visibility | Multi-matter invoices (Onit Q2 2026), dynamic discounting (Litify), DaaS warehouse (Onit) |
| Standard dashboards/reports; audit log; SSO | NL Q&A assistants (TR Aug 2025, Mitratech ARIES), MCP connectors (Brightflag/LawVu 2026), anomaly detection, AI projections |
| Outlook: approve from the reading pane / file email | Teams app, Copilot plugin (LawVu 2024–2026; actions still pending), agentic workflow builders (LawVu LegalOS June 2026) |
| — | Accruals and budgets are usually **present in v1 for incumbents but are the first things new entrants defer**: CounselAudit ships budgets/WIP collection; Brightflag's TEI credits accrual productivity; PERSUIT replaces accrual requests with streamed WIP |

**Read-across to the Spaarke r1 plan.** The planned scope (PDF-first portal/mailbox/Outlook intake → AI header + line extraction → OCG/rate-card/procedure documents → AI rules → line checks with LEDES/UTBMS → immediate rejection + firm notification → in-house approve/adjust) covers every item in the left column except LEDES *upload* (planned "later"), and includes two right-column items (OCG-document ingestion; email/Outlook intake) that are the differentiators. Two left-column items are missing from the stated r1 scope and buyers will ask for them early: **timekeeper/rate approval that gates submission** (CounselLink/Legal Tracker pattern; also the simplest "immediate rejection" rule) and **AP hand-off / payment status for firms**. An appeal loop can be a lightweight "dispute with note → re-review" in r1, with Passport-style partial payment deferred.
