# Alder County Office of the County Attorney: Outside Counsel Billing Guidelines

**Alder County Office of the County Attorney** · Version 3.1 · Effective 2026-01-01

> Synthetic document written for Spaarke's billing-guideline evaluation. The organisation is fictional.

These guidelines apply to every law firm the County retains. They are part of each engagement contract; where the contract and these guidelines differ, the contract controls.

## 1. Engagement and conduct

**1.1** Before accepting a matter, the firm confirms in writing that it has no conflict with the County, its elected officials or its departments, and discloses any relationship that could appear to be one.

**1.2** Within 30 days of assignment the firm sends the assigned Assistant County Attorney an initial case assessment with a litigation plan and an estimate of fees through trial.

**1.3** The firm does not speak to the media about a County matter without the County Attorney's prior approval.

**1.4** Settlement offers are reported to the County promptly. Only the Board of Commissioners, or an official it delegates, may authorise a settlement.

## 2. Recording time

**2.1** Time is recorded contemporaneously in increments of one-tenth of an hour (0.1 hour). An entry recorded in any other increment is reduced to the next lower tenth of an hour.

**2.2** Each task is a separate entry with its own time and a description specific enough to show what was done and why. Block billing, meaning several tasks under one time charge, and vague descriptions such as "attention to file" or "review documents" are not acceptable. Such entries are referred to the assigned Assistant County Attorney for review.

**2.3** Clerical and administrative work is overhead and is not billable, whoever performs it. It includes scheduled conference calls, calendar entries, recording docketed deadlines, conflict checks, invoice preparation, preparing photocopied exhibits, and documents organized and indexed for the file. Entries for this work are disallowed and not paid.

## 3. Expenses

**3.1** Expenses are billed at actual cost, without markup.

**3.2** Word processing (UTBMS expense code E103), facsimile charges (E104) and online legal research such as Westlaw or Lexis (E106) are overhead. They are not reimbursed and are disallowed.

**3.3** In-house photocopying (E101) is reimbursed at no more than $0.15 per page. A charge above that rate is reduced to $0.15 per page.

**3.4** The firm keeps a receipt for any single expense over $50 and produces it on request.

## 4. Invoices

**4.1** Invoices are submitted monthly.

**4.2** The County must receive each invoice within 45 days after the end of the billing period it covers. A later invoice is referred for review.

**4.3** Each invoice shows the County purchase order (PO) number issued for the matter. An invoice without a PO number is returned unpaid.

**4.4** An entry that repeats another entry for the same timekeeper, date, hours and description is a duplicate and is disallowed.

## 5. Review and payment

**5.1** When an entry or invoice does not comply with these guidelines, the outcome is the one the clause states: the amount is reduced to what the clause allows; the charge is disallowed and not paid; the item is referred for review, which may lead to a question to the firm or an adjustment; or the whole invoice is returned unpaid for correction and resubmission.

**5.2** The County pays approved invoices within 45 days of approval.

**5.3** The County may audit the records supporting any invoice for three years after final payment.
