# Kestrel Mutual Insurance Company: Litigation and Coverage Counsel Billing Guidelines

**Kestrel Mutual Insurance Company, Claims Legal** · Version 2026 · Effective 2026-01-01

> Synthetic document written for Spaarke's billing-guideline evaluation. The organisation is fictional.

These guidelines apply to counsel Kestrel Mutual (the Company) assigns to defend its insureds or to represent the Company in coverage and other litigation. Nothing in them limits counsel's independent professional judgement or duty to the insured.

## 1. Reporting

**1.1** Within 45 days of assignment counsel sends the claims professional an initial report with an evaluation and a budget through trial.

**1.2** Counsel sends a pre-trial report at least 60 days before trial.

**1.3** Legal research of more than 3 hours on one issue needs the claims professional's approval in advance.

## 2. Rates

**2.1** Hourly rates may not exceed the maximum for the timekeeper's classification in Appendix A. Time billed at a higher rate is reduced to the Appendix A maximum.

## 3. Recording time

**3.1** Time is recorded in increments of one-tenth of an hour (0.1 hour), rounded down. An entry in any other increment is reduced to the next lower tenth.

**3.2** Each task is recorded separately with a description that shows what was done. Block-billed entries and vague descriptions (for example "work on file" or "trial preparation" with no detail) are reduced.

**3.3** Clerical and administrative work is not billable, including scheduled conference calls, calendar entries, recording docketed deadlines, conflict checks, invoice preparation, preparing photocopied exhibits, and documents organized and indexed for the file. Internal conferences between counsel's own lawyers or staff are not billable (UTBMS activity code A105, Communicate (within the firm)). Such entries are disallowed.

**3.4** Time of more than 12 hours by one timekeeper in one day is referred to the claims professional for review.

## 4. Expenses

**4.1** Expenses are billed at actual cost. Counsel keeps receipts for any item over $25 and produces them on request.

**4.2** Experts and investigators are retained only with the claims professional's written approval.

## 5. Invoices

**5.1** Invoices are submitted in LEDES format. Every time entry carries a UTBMS task code and activity code, and every invoice shows the purchase order (PO) number on the assignment letter. An invoice that lacks a code on any time entry, or lacks the PO number, is returned unpaid.

**5.2** Charges for work performed more than 120 days before the Company receives the invoice are disallowed.

**5.3** An entry that repeats another entry for the same timekeeper, date, hours and description is a duplicate and is disallowed.

**5.4** Invoices under $1,000 are held and submitted quarterly unless they are the final invoice on the matter.

**5.5** When an entry or invoice does not comply with these guidelines, the outcome is the one the clause states: the amount is reduced to what the clause allows; the charge is disallowed and not paid; the item is referred for review, which may lead to a question to the firm or an adjustment; or the whole invoice is returned unpaid for correction and resubmission.

**5.6** Counsel may appeal an adjustment within 14 days of receiving it.
