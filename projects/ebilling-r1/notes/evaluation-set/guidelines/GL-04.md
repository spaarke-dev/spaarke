# Harborline Financial Corporation: Preferred Counsel Panel Billing Guidelines

**Harborline Financial Corporation, Legal Department** · Version Panel term 2025-2026 · Effective 2025-01-01

> Synthetic document written for Spaarke's billing-guideline evaluation. The organisation is fictional.

These guidelines apply to every firm on Harborline's Preferred Counsel Panel and form part of the Panel Agreement. Panel firms handle Harborline's litigation, regulatory and employment matters.

## 1. Panel membership and conduct

**1.1** Panel firms meet Harborline's vendor risk requirements and sign the annual information security attestation.

**1.2** The firm tells the Legal Department within 24 hours if a regulator contacts it about a Harborline matter.

**1.3** Panel firms commit to staff Harborline matters with diverse teams and report on them each year.

## 2. Staffing

**2.1** For each matter the Legal Department approves an Approved Timekeeper List. Only timekeepers on that list may bill the matter. An invoice that includes time by any other timekeeper is returned unpaid.

**2.2** Time spent bringing a new team member up to speed is not charged without the Legal Department's agreement.

## 3. Rates

**3.1** Hourly rates may not exceed the maximum for the timekeeper's classification in Appendix A. Time billed at a higher rate is reduced to the Appendix A maximum.

**3.2** Rates are fixed for the panel term; no increase takes effect during it.

## 4. Recording time

**4.1** As provided in the Panel Agreement, time is recorded in quarter-hour increments (0.25 hour). An entry in any other increment is reduced to the next lower quarter hour.

**4.2** Clerical and administrative work is not billable. It includes scheduled conference calls, calendar entries, recording docketed deadlines, conflict checks, invoice preparation, preparing photocopied exhibits, and documents organized and indexed for the file (UTBMS activity code A110, Manage data/files). Such entries are disallowed.

## 5. Expenses

**5.1** Facsimile charges (UTBMS expense code E104) and online legal research (E106) are not reimbursed. Photocopying (E101) charged at more than $0.15 per page is not reimbursed. These charges are disallowed.

**5.2** Experts, consultants and other vendors costing more than $10,000 need the Legal Department's approval before they are engaged.

## 6. Invoices and budgets

**6.1** Harborline must receive each invoice within 60 days after the end of the billing period. A later invoice is referred for review.

**6.2** Work must fall within the matter's open and close dates as recorded by the Legal Department; time or expenses dated outside them are referred for review. An invoice whose total exceeds the approved matter budget is referred for review.

**6.3** An entry that repeats another entry for the same timekeeper, date, hours and description is a duplicate and is disallowed.

**6.4** The firm submits an accrual of unbilled fees for each open matter by the fifth business day after quarter end.

**6.5** When an entry or invoice does not comply with these guidelines, the outcome is the one the clause states: the amount is reduced to what the clause allows; the charge is disallowed and not paid; the item is referred for review, which may lead to a question to the firm or an adjustment; or the whole invoice is returned unpaid for correction and resubmission.
