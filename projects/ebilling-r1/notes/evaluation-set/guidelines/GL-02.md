# Lakemont State University System Office of General Counsel: Outside Counsel Guidelines

**Lakemont State University System, Office of General Counsel** · Version 2025-26 · Effective 2025-07-01

> Synthetic document written for Spaarke's billing-guideline evaluation. The organisation is fictional.

The Office of General Counsel (OGC) alone retains outside counsel for the System and its campuses. These guidelines apply to every engagement and are incorporated in each engagement letter.

## 1. Engagement

**1.1** No campus, department or employee may retain outside counsel for the System; only the OGC may.

**1.2** The firm discloses any conflict before the engagement and any that arises during it.

**1.3** Student education records and patient health information are confidential under federal law. The firm notifies the OGC within 24 hours of any incident that may expose System data.

## 2. Staffing

**2.1** With the engagement letter the firm submits a staffing plan naming each lawyer and paraprofessional who will work on the matter and their classification. The plan, once the OGC approves it, is the firm's approved roster; adding a timekeeper needs the OGC's written approval first. An invoice that includes time by anyone not on the approved roster is returned unpaid.

**2.2** The OGC expects lean staffing and may ask the firm to explain the staffing of any task.

## 3. Rates

**3.1** Rates billed to the System are at least 10% below the firm's standard rates for the same timekeepers.

**3.2** Each timekeeper's hourly rate is the rate in the firm's approved rate schedule, which may not exceed the maximum for the timekeeper's classification in Appendix A. Rates are fixed for the fiscal year (July to June) and change only when the OGC issues a revised schedule. Time billed at a higher rate is reduced to the approved rate.

## 4. Recording time

**4.1** Time is recorded in increments of one-tenth of an hour (0.1 hour). An entry in any other increment is reduced to the next lower tenth.

**4.2** No timekeeper may bill more than 10 hours in a single day on System matters. Time above 10 hours in a day is reduced to 10 hours.

## 5. Invoices

**5.1** Invoices are submitted in LEDES format. Every time entry carries a UTBMS litigation task code (L100 to L500 series) and an activity code (A100 series). An invoice with a time entry that lacks its codes is returned unpaid.

**5.2** Each invoice covers one matter.

**5.3** The OGC must receive each invoice within 30 days after the end of the billing period. A later invoice is referred for review. Any charge for work performed more than 90 days before the OGC receives the invoice is also referred for review.

**5.4** Time and expenses must fall between the date the OGC opens the matter and the date it closes it; an entry dated outside those dates is referred for review. An invoice whose total exceeds the approved budget for the matter is referred for review.

**5.5** An entry that repeats another entry for the same timekeeper, date, hours and description is a duplicate and is disallowed.

**5.6** The firm sends a budget within 30 days of the engagement and an update whenever it expects fees to exceed the budget by more than 10%.

## 6. Review and payment

**6.1** When an entry or invoice does not comply with these guidelines, the outcome is the one the clause states: the amount is reduced to what the clause allows; the charge is disallowed and not paid; the item is referred for review, which may lead to a question to the firm or an adjustment; or the whole invoice is returned unpaid for correction and resubmission.

**6.2** The System pays approved invoices within 45 days. The OGC reviews each firm's performance annually.
