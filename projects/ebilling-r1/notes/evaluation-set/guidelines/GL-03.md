# Brennick Industrial Group, Inc. Law Department: Outside Counsel Billing Requirements

**Brennick Industrial Group, Inc., Law Department** · Version 4 · Effective 2026-03-01

> Synthetic document written for Spaarke's billing-guideline evaluation. The organisation is fictional.

These requirements apply to all legal services bought by Brennick Industrial Group and its subsidiaries (the Company), including product liability, commercial, employment and environmental matters. Outside counsel buys are managed by the Law Department with Procurement.

## 1. Working with the Law Department

**1.1** The responsible in-house lawyer directs strategy. The firm sends that lawyer a short status report each month on every open matter.

**1.2** The firm follows the Company's Supplier Code of Conduct and returns the Company's information security questionnaire within 10 business days of the request.

**1.3** The firm reports annually on the diversity of the teams staffing Company matters.

## 2. Rates

**2.1** Hourly rates may not exceed the maximum for the timekeeper's classification in Schedule 1. Time billed at a higher rate is reduced to the Schedule 1 maximum.

**2.2** Rates may change at most once a year, on 90 days' written notice, and by no more than 3%.

## 3. Recording time

**3.1** Time is recorded in increments of one-tenth of an hour (0.1 hour). An entry in any other increment is reduced to the next lower tenth.

**3.2** No timekeeper may bill more than 8 hours in one day on Company matters. Time above 8 hours in a day is reduced to 8 hours.

**3.3** Each task is recorded separately with its own time. Block billing, several tasks under one time charge, is not accepted; block-billed entries are referred to the Law Department for review.

**3.4** The following are not billable and are disallowed: clerical and administrative work, including scheduled conference calls, calendar entries, recording docketed deadlines, conflict checks, invoice preparation, preparing photocopied exhibits, and documents organized and indexed for the file (UTBMS activity code A110, Manage data/files); and time spent in internal conferences between lawyers or staff of the firm (UTBMS activity code A105, Communicate (within the firm)).

**3.5** Legal research of more than 5 hours on a single issue needs the responsible in-house lawyer's approval in advance.

## 4. Expenses

**4.1** Word processing (UTBMS expense code E103) and online legal research (E106) are part of the firm's overhead and are disallowed.

**4.2** Photocopying (E101) is reimbursed at no more than $0.10 per page, and meals (E111) at no more than $60.00 per meal. Amounts above those limits are reduced to the limit.

**4.3** Travel needs advance approval. Air travel is reimbursed at economy fares only.

## 5. Invoices and payment

**5.1** Procurement issues a purchase order (PO) for each matter. Every invoice shows that PO number; an invoice without it is returned unpaid.

**5.2** Invoices are submitted monthly through the Company's e-billing system.

**5.3** When an entry or invoice does not comply with these guidelines, the outcome is the one the clause states: the amount is reduced to what the clause allows; the charge is disallowed and not paid; the item is referred for review, which may lead to a question to the firm or an adjustment; or the whole invoice is returned unpaid for correction and resubmission.

**5.4** The Company pays approved invoices 60 days after approval.
