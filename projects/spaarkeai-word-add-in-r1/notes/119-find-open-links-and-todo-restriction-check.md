## Part B - To Do on a restricted record (no code changed)
- `sprk_todo` DOES have its own `sprk_accesspermission` (Standard/Limited/Restricted, live describe). No server code writes that column on a child. Readers are the root-table checks (`SecureChildShareSynchronizer.cs:1019-1026`, `OfficeEditAccessService.cs:235`). `docs/data-model/sprk_communication.md:12` states the rule for communications: a child inherits its ROOT's permission and carries none of its own. The UAC cascade note's claim that the column is not on sprk_todo is stale.
- Our path, `OfficeService.CreateTodoAsync` (`Services/Office/OfficeService.cs:2067`), DOES call the shared platform logic: the core-ancestor stamp for the regarding record (`_coreAncestors.StampAsync`, fail-closed) and for a carrier-only To Do (`ClassifyStampSource`), and the shared owner resolver `ResolveTodoOwnerTeamAsync` -> `_ownershipResolver.ResolveOwnerAsync` with secure-if-any (`RecordOwnershipResolver`, task 146). A To Do under a SECURE parent is therefore owned by the Secure team. It never sets `sprk_accesspermission`, so the field stays at its default, Standard.
- "Secure" (`sprk_issecure`, Secure-team ownership) is handled. "Restricted" (`sprk_accesspermission` = 100000002 on a root) is a root-level flag that no path copies down to a To Do (`ChildRecordEndpoints` does not either), so a Standard To Do under a Restricted record is current platform behaviour, not an Office-only gap.
- Recommendation: UAC-r2 owns the decision. Preferred long-term: treat the To Do like a communication - inherit from the root (retire or lock the To Do column and show the root's permission) rather than stamping a copy. If the owner wants the column populated, do it once in the shared child-create/ownership path (RecordOwnershipResolver / CoreAncestorResolver stamp) so Office, wizard and ChildRecordEndpoints all get it; an Office-only write would repeat the divergence. Confirm with UAC-r2 that the "Restricted" the owner expected is the To Do's own column.
