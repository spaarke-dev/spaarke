## Item 2 — Outlook desktop steps given to the owner (2026-10-06)

The laptop that works runs the **new** Outlook (Apps flyout, "Apps" pane header). On the desktop, in order:

1. **Which Outlook?** If the desktop shows the *New Outlook* toggle at the top right, it is classic — switch the toggle on and try Spaarke from Apps. If it works there, the package is fine and the problem is classic-only on that machine.
2. **New Outlook, reset its local state**: quit Outlook (no `olk.exe` in Task Manager) → Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → *Outlook (new)* → ⋯ → Advanced options → **Reset** → reopen and sign in.
3. **Classic Outlook, clear the add-in cache**: close Outlook → delete the contents of `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Office\16.0\Wef\` → reopen. Also check File → Office Account → About Outlook; a build older than the laptop's may not load the unified package.
4. Still failing → send: Outlook flavor + version, whether Spaarke shows in the Apps flyout, and what happens on click.
