# Playbook: Spaarke NDA – Mutual v1.0
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Spaarke Legal Engineering | Effective: 2026-11-01 | Jurisdiction default: Delaware | Side: mutual | Status: POC test positions, not attorney-approved

These are balanced positions for a mutual NDA where each party both discloses and receives. They
follow the structure of the Common Paper Mutual NDA (CC BY 4.0) and general U.S. practice; the
wording is ours. Because each party is on both sides, positions favour symmetry and workable
obligations over maximum protection. Topics this playbook does not cover are reported as silent.

Scope. Agreement family: non-disclosure agreement where each party discloses and receives. Represented side: mutual (apply every position to both parties). Commercial context: standard commercial discussions, not M&A. Jurisdiction: United States; Delaware default. Negotiation stage: acceptable end state; fallbacks in order. Do not use for: one-way NDAs, M&A confidentiality agreements, employee confidentiality agreements.

## topic: nda.definition-unmarked
Preferred: Confidential Information covers information identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.
Fallback: Marking required, with written designation within 30 days for information disclosed unmarked or orally.
Prohibited / escalate: Marking-only definition with no designation window.
Model language: ", or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of its disclosure"
Rationale: A failure to mark should not forfeit protection; a reasonable-person test is market standard for mutual NDAs.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.oral-disclosure
Preferred: Oral and visual disclosures are protected when identified as confidential at the time or reasonably understood to be confidential.
Fallback: Protected if summarised in writing within 30 days.
Prohibited / escalate: Oral disclosures excluded entirely.
Rationale: Much early-stage diligence is oral.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.exclusion-prior-possession
Preferred: Excluded if the recipient knew it without a duty of confidentiality before disclosure, shown by its records.
Fallback: Excluded if already known to the recipient before disclosure.
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Rationale: Standard exclusion; the records requirement keeps it provable.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.exclusion-independent-development
Preferred: Excluded if independently developed without use of or reference to the other party's Confidential Information.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Independent development without the "without use of" qualifier.
Model language: "without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information"
Rationale: Protects legitimate parallel work while preventing laundering of disclosed information.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.exclusion-third-party
Preferred: Excluded if rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.
Fallback: Excluded if received from a third party without breach known to the recipient.
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Rationale: Standard exclusion.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.one-sided-carveouts
Preferred: No exclusions beyond the standard four, and every exclusion applies equally to both parties.
Fallback: A narrow, defined carve-out that applies to both parties.
Prohibited / escalate: A carve-out that removes a class of only one party's information.
Rationale: A mutual NDA should be mutual in substance.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.purpose-limited-use
Preferred: Use only for the stated Purpose; protect with at least reasonable care.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: No purpose limit, or use "for any lawful purpose".
Model language: "solely for the Purpose"
Rationale: Use restriction is the core of an NDA.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.representatives
Preferred: Disclosure to Representatives with a need to know who are bound by obligations at least as protective; each party is responsible for its Representatives' breaches.
Fallback: Representatives bound by written or professional obligations of confidentiality.
Prohibited / escalate: No responsibility for Representatives.
Model language: "Each party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives."
Rationale: Keeps a remedy against the party that chose to share.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.compelled-disclosure
Preferred: Prompt notice where legally permitted, reasonable cooperation at the disclosing party's expense, and disclosure limited to what is legally required.
Fallback: Notice where legally permitted.
Prohibited / escalate: Recipient may disclose on its own judgment with no notice.
Rationale: Gives the owner a chance to seek a protective order.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.reverse-engineering
Preferred: No reverse engineering, decompiling or analysing the composition of tangible materials disclosed.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Silent where prototypes, samples or software are disclosed.
Rationale: Relevant whenever tangible items change hands.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.copying-and-breach-notice
Preferred: Copies only as needed for the Purpose, with notices retained; prompt notice of unauthorised use or disclosure.
Fallback: Prompt notice of unauthorised disclosure.
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Rationale: Breach notice lets the owner mitigate.
Priority: Low

## topic: nda.data-use-licence-back
Preferred: No use of the other party's information, or data derived from it, for any purpose other than the Purpose.
Fallback: Aggregated data that cannot identify either party or reveal its Confidential Information, used only to improve the services provided to the other party.
Prohibited / escalate: Use of derived, anonymised or aggregated data for the recipient's general product development or for third parties.
Rationale: Derived-data clauses quietly turn an NDA into a licence.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.no-licence-no-warranty
Preferred: No licence is granted except to use for the Purpose; information is provided as is.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: An implied licence or a warranty of accuracy given by either party.
Rationale: Standard and symmetric.
Priority: Low

## topic: nda.return-destruction
Preferred: Return or destroy within 30 days of request or termination, with certification on request; routine backups and legally required copies may be kept, still subject to confidentiality.
Fallback: Return or destroy on request, without a deadline.
Prohibited / escalate: No obligation to return or destroy.
Rationale: Workable for both parties; the backup exception reflects how systems work.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.residuals
Preferred: No residuals clause.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Any residuals clause allowing use of information retained in unaided memory.
Rationale: Residuals clauses can swallow the protection; escalate rather than negotiate wording.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.term
Preferred: Disclosures made during a 1- to 2-year disclosure period are protected for 3 years after disclosure; trade secrets are protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.
Fallback: 2 years after disclosure, plus trade secrets for as long as they qualify.
Prohibited / escalate: Obligations end with the agreement, or trade secrets lose protection at a fixed date.
Model language: "provided that obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law"
Rationale: Balanced term for a mutual NDA; trade secrets need an open-ended tail.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.non-solicit
Preferred: No non-solicit or non-compete in an NDA.
Fallback: Mutual non-solicit of employees the party met through the discussions, 12 months, with a general-solicitation exception.
Prohibited / escalate: Any non-compete; a non-solicit longer than 12 months or without the general-solicitation exception.
Rationale: Restrictive covenants belong in the deal agreement and can be unenforceable in some states.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.remedies
Preferred: Breach may cause irreparable harm; either party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Waiver of injunctive relief, or a liability cap that applies to confidentiality breaches.
Rationale: Symmetric remedies; "may seek" is acceptable in a mutual NDA.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.whistleblower-dtsa
Preferred: Nothing prevents reporting to a government agency; where an individual is bound, include the 18 U.S.C. 1833(b) immunity notice.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Language that restricts reporting to regulators.
Rationale: Preserves statutory rights; the DTSA notice preserves exemplary damages and fee recovery against individuals. [verify] applicability per party type.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.trade-secret-preservation
Preferred: Nothing in the NDA limits either party's rights and remedies for trade secrets under applicable law, and trade-secret obligations outlast the fixed term.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Language disclaiming obligations implied by law, or ending trade-secret protection at a fixed date.
Rationale: An NDA that caps trade-secret protection can undermine the "reasonable measures" a trade secret requires.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.assignment
Preferred: No assignment without consent, except to a successor of all or substantially all of the relevant business that assumes the agreement.
Fallback: Consent not to be unreasonably withheld.
Prohibited / escalate: Free assignment.
Rationale: Symmetric and market standard.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.governing-law-venue
Preferred: Delaware law; courts of Delaware, non-exclusive.
Fallback: The law of either party's home state, with non-exclusive venue.
Prohibited / escalate: Exclusive venue in a jurisdiction neither party is in.
Rationale: Neutral default for a mutual NDA; venue should not be a weapon.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.confidentiality-of-agreement
Preferred: The existence and terms of the discussions are Confidential Information of both parties.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Rationale: Protects deal confidentiality.
Priority: Low

## topic: nda.housekeeping
Preferred: Effective date, legal names, notice details and signatory authority confirmed.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Prior signature artefacts or another deal's names left in the document.
Rationale: Business confirmations: comment, do not edit.
Priority: Low
