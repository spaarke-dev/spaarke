## topic: nda.definition-unmarked
Preferred: Retain the definition: non-public information disclosed in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is either marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Keep the reasonable-person limb; reject any requirement that information must be marked to be protected. Keep oral and visual disclosures covered without a written-summary requirement.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Reject any expansion to information not disclosed in connection with the Purpose.
Model language: "2.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of a party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, in any form, that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of its disclosure. 2.2 Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information if it is identified as confidential when disclosed or would reasonably be understood to be confidential."

## topic: nda.exclusion-prior-possession
Preferred: Retain all four standard exclusions: prior knowledge, public availability, receipt from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, and independent development. Keep the written-records evidence requirement on prior knowledge, the "through no fault of the Receiving Party" qualifier on public availability, and the "without use of or reference to" test on independent development.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Escalate any added exclusion. Reject deletion of the evidence and fault qualifiers.
Model language: "3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) was known to the Receiving Party without a duty of confidentiality before its disclosure, as shown by the Receiving Party's written records; (b) is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information."

## topic: nda.purpose-limited-use
Preferred: Retain use solely for the Purpose and a standard of care of at least the care used for the party's own similar information, and in no event less than reasonable care. Keep the express bar on using Confidential Information or data derived from it for any other purpose.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Reject a standard limited to the party's own practices with no reasonable-care floor, and reject any carve-out permitting use of derived data.
Model language: "4.1 The Receiving Party will use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and will protect it using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care. 4.5 Neither party will use the other party's Confidential Information, or any data derived from it, for any purpose other than the Purpose."

## topic: nda.representatives
Preferred: Retain disclosure only to directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as the NDA. Keep each party responsible for breaches by its Representatives.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Escalate any request to add other recipients (for example affiliates, financing sources or potential acquirers). Reject deletion of the responsibility sentence.
Model language: "4.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its directors, officers, employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose ("Representatives") and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement. Each party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives."
Rationale: Imported from cowork-create-playbook.docx, row 6 (Disclosure to Representatives). Mapped by keyword (score 22); confirm.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.compelled-disclosure
Preferred: Retain the compelled-disclosure mechanism: prompt written notice to the extent legally permitted, reasonable cooperation at the Disclosing Party's expense in seeking a protective order, and disclosure limited to the portion legally required.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Reject any change that shifts the cost of cooperation to the Receiving Party, removes the notice duty, or widens the trigger beyond law, regulation or court order.
Model language: "5.1 If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it will, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, reasonably cooperate, at the Disclosing Party's expense, with any effort to obtain a protective order, and disclose only the portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose."
Rationale: Imported from cowork-create-playbook.docx, row 9 (Compelled disclosure). Mapped by keyword (score 25); confirm.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.return-destruction
Preferred: Retain the obligation to return or destroy within thirty (30) days after written request or termination, with written certification on request. Keep the carve-out for routine backup copies and copies required by law, and keep those retained copies subject to the agreement.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Reject an unconditional destruction obligation with no backup carve-out, and reject any carve-out that releases retained copies from confidentiality.
Model language: "7.1 Within thirty (30) days after the Disclosing Party's written request, or after this Agreement terminates, the Receiving Party will return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and, on request, certify in writing that it has done so. The Receiving Party may keep copies in routine backup systems or as required by law, and those copies remain subject to this Agreement."

## topic: nda.term
Preferred: Hold confidentiality obligations at three (3) years after each disclosure, measured per disclosure rather than from the Effective Date. Keep the trade-secret proviso so trade-secret obligations last as long as the information remains a trade secret. If the counterparty asks for a shorter period, apply the compromise position; escalate any request below that or any request to drop the trade-secret proviso.
Fallback: Two (2) years after each disclosure, with the trade-secret proviso retained unchanged: "The Receiving Party's obligations continue for two (2) years after each disclosure, provided that obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law."
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Model language: "8.1 This Agreement covers disclosures made during the two (2) years following the Effective Date. The Receiving Party's obligations continue for three (3) years after each disclosure, provided that obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law."
Rationale: Imported from cowork-create-playbook.docx, row 13 (Duration of confidentiality obligations). Mapped by keyword (score 10); confirm.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.non-solicit
Preferred: Do not include a non-solicitation clause in Acme's draft. If the counterparty asks for one, use only the compromise wording: mutual, twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, limited to employees the soliciting party had contact with in connection with the Purpose, with a carve-out for general solicitations and resulting hires.
Fallback: If requested by the counterparty, accept this mutual wording: "For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither party will solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. General solicitations not targeted at those employees, and hiring anyone who responds to them, do not breach this section."
Prohibited / escalate: Escalate any broader request, such as a no-hire clause, a longer period, or coverage of all employees.
Rationale: Imported from cowork-create-playbook.docx, row 15 (Non-solicitation of employees). Mapped by keyword (score 21); confirm.
Priority: High
