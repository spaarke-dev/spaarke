# Route authorization sweep (2026-10-02): sign-in-only routes acting app-only on caller-chosen records

> **Trigger:** task 156's verifier found that all 8 `/api/v1/events` routes are guarded only by sign-in while acting through an app-only client (#1098). A read-only workflow (`uac-idor-sweep`, run `wf_c2daa097-2f4`, 94 agents) then traced **every route in `src/server/api/Sprk.Bff.Api/Api/`** on the work branch (master `5e1fdcc0b` plus test-only changes). One finder covered each slice, and every finding went to an independent skeptic told to REFUTE it: find a missed filter, policy, access probe, OBO client, caller-scoped query or unmapped feature flag.

**Routes traced:** 419 (ai-a 58, ai-b 66, admin-spe 79, toplevel 95, external-membership 47, workspace-misc 74).

**Results:**
- **Confirmed in the class: 82** (critical 17, high 29, medium 30, low 6).
- **Refuted by the skeptic: 6.**
- Noteworthy but not in the class: 62.

**The class.** A route is in it when all three hold:
1. its authorization is only "signed in";
2. its data access is app-only, through the BFF managed identity (`DataverseServiceClientImpl`, `DataverseWebApiService`, AI Search with the service credential, or SPE as the app);
3. the caller chooses the record (a route id, a body id, a regarding filter, FetchXML or an index partition).

The effect: Dataverse row security, secure isolation and the ethical walls do not apply to the caller.

**Read-only.** The sweep changed no code, configuration or live data.
