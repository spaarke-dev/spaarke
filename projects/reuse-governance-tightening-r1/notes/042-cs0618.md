## What changed

A comment in its place says that CS0618 is an error again: marking a member `[Obsolete]` fails every existing caller, so the marking change migrates them or uses a narrow `DiagnosticId` suppression with a reason, and CS0618 itself is never re-suppressed.

- `OntologyWriterCredentialFactory.cs` (BFF): this was a framework obsoletion, fixed in well under an hour. `DefaultAzureCredentialOptions.ExcludeSharedTokenCacheCredential` has been `[Obsolete]` since Azure.Identity 1.15 and defaults to `true`. Behaviour is unchanged: `OntologyWriterCredentialFactoryTests.BuildOptions_ExcludesEveryCredentialSourceExceptManagedIdentity` enumerates every `Exclude*` property by reflection and still asserts it is `true` (passes).
