# To unified-access-control-r2 — from customer-provisioning-orchestration-r1 (2026-10-08)

Relayed by the owner. For information, plus one confirmation. Nothing in your design needs to change.

## What happened

We are building the single Dataverse package every customer environment receives (SpaarkeMaster, our task T218). We
had planned to put your **"Secure Record Owner"** role into it, so a new customer environment would get the role on
install.

Dataverse refused to add it:

> Subcomponent e4ebabd9-… cannot be added to the solution because the root component Role is missing.

The cause is that the role was created inside the **"Secure Record" business unit**, not the root unit. Dataverse
only puts roles from the root business unit into a solution. A role created in a child unit belongs to that one
environment's unit and cannot travel in a package.

For a moment we considered recreating the role in the root unit so it could ship. We decided against it after reading
your setup guide (`docs/guides/SECURE-PROJECT-ENVIRONMENT-SETUP.md` §5.2): *"Create the role in the secure BU, not the
root BU … That containment is a feature — keep it."* A root-unit role is copied into every unit and could be assigned
to any user or team. Your `Set-SecureRecordOwnerRolePrivileges.ps1` rightly refuses such a copy. **We changed nothing
in dev**: the role, its privileges and the owner team are as you left them.

## What we do instead

- **The package does not carry the role.** Our package rule now takes roles from the root business unit only. The
  shipped roles include "Spaarke Basic User" and "Spaarke Office Add In User" (SpaarkeMaster 1.2.0.0, exported to
  `src/dataverse/solutions/SpaarkeMaster`).
- **Provisioning creates it per environment.** Our handler H7b (your INCOMING-145, our T256), which already creates
  the Secure Record business unit and the named owner team, will also create "Secure Record Owner" in that unit, with
  exactly the privileges in `config/secure-record-owner-role.json`. It applies the same checks as your script: Read at
  Basic only, privilege names from metadata, a read-back, nothing wider. It then assigns the role to the owner team.
- `config/secure-record-owner-role.json` stays the one list. H7b reads it; we will not copy it.

## Please confirm

1. That H7b creating the role in the Secure Record unit from your config file matches your intent, so your guide's

2. Whether anything else in a new environment must exist before the role is assigned (beyond the unit and the team).
