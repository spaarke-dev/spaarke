# Playbook: NDA - Disclosing Party (Acme sample customer)
Version: 1.0 | Owner: Acme Legal (sample) | Effective: 2026-11-01 | Jurisdiction default: Florida | Side: disclosing | Status: SAMPLE customer playbook for MVP testing, not legal advice

Sample customer playbook used to test that a customer's own playbook overrides the Spaarke
baseline. Positions are the aggressive disclosing-party positions from the 2026-10-30 POC session.

## topic: nda.definition-unmarked
Preferred: Confidential Information includes information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, whether or not marked.
Fallback: Marking required, but with a 30-day written-designation cure for unmarked and oral disclosures.
Prohibited / escalate: Marking-only definition with no cure.
Model language: ", or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of its disclosure"
Rationale: Inadvertent failure to mark should not forfeit protection.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.oral-disclosure
Preferred: Oral disclosures protected if identified as, or reasonably understood to be, confidential.
Fallback: Written summary within 30 days.
Prohibited / escalate: Must be identified as "Confidential Information" at the time of disclosure.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.exclusion-prior-possession
Preferred: Excluded only if free of any confidentiality obligation, shown by contemporaneous written records.
Fallback: Shown by written records.
Prohibited / escalate: Bare "already in possession".
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.exclusion-third-party
Preferred: Third party lawfully entitled to disclose without restriction.
Fallback: Without breach known to the recipient.
Prohibited / escalate: n/a
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.one-sided-carveouts
Preferred: None.
Fallback: A narrow, defined carve-out.
Prohibited / escalate: Carve-outs removing classes of our information (for example patent numbers).
Priority: High

## topic: nda.compelled-disclosure
Preferred: Prompt written notice where legally permitted; disclose only what counsel advises in writing is required; cooperate.
Fallback: Prompt notice.
Prohibited / escalate: Recipient decides scope alone.
Priority: Low

## topic: nda.representatives
Preferred: Recipient responsible for its representatives' breaches.
Fallback: Representatives bound by written obligations.
Prohibited / escalate: No responsibility.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.reverse-engineering
Preferred: Prohibited.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Silent while tangible materials are disclosed.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.copying-and-breach-notice
Preferred: Copying only as necessary; prompt written breach notice and cooperation.
Fallback: Breach notice only.
Prohibited / escalate: Neither.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.data-use-licence-back
Preferred: None.
Fallback: Recipient's own information only.
Prohibited / escalate: Any internal R&D or derived-data use of our information.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.return-destruction
Preferred: Within 10 days of request, with written certification.
Fallback: Within 30 days.
Prohibited / escalate: No deadline.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.term
Preferred: 5 years from disclosure; trade secrets for as long as they qualify.
Fallback: 3 years plus trade secrets.
Prohibited / escalate: "Lesser of" formulas; termination on signing any deal.
Model language: "five (5) years from the date of disclosure"
Priority: High

## topic: nda.remedies
Preferred: Irreparable harm acknowledged; injunctive relief without bond or proof of damages.
Fallback: Injunctive relief available.
Prohibited / escalate: "May" seek only.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.assignment
Preferred: Consent required.
Fallback: Consent except to a successor of the whole business.
Prohibited / escalate: Free assignment.
Priority: Medium

## topic: nda.governing-law-venue
Preferred: Florida law; Hillsborough County or M.D. Fla. (Tampa).
Fallback: Delaware.
Prohibited / escalate: Counterparty home state with exclusive venue.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.trade-secret-preservation
Preferred: Nothing limits trade-secret rights and remedies.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Disclaims obligations implied by law.
Priority: High

## topic: nda.housekeeping
Preferred: Effective date, legal names and signatory confirmed.
Fallback: n/a
Prohibited / escalate: Prior signature artefacts embedded.
Priority: Low
