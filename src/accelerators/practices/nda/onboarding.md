# Onboarding module: NDA

Copied into `accelerator-setup/references/onboarding-nda.md` at build time.

## Extra questions (Full path only)

1. Which NDAs do you see most? Inbound from counterparties / Our own paper / Both
2. Any positions you always hold? (free text: for example "never accept residuals", "Florida law")
   Record them in the profile under "House positions"; do not edit the playbook here.
3. Who usually signs? (free text, for the housekeeping check)

## Recommended skills

| Skill | Why |
|---|---|
| nda-review | Packaged redline set against your playbook |
| Legal for Cowork review-contract | Non-NDA contracts |
| Word legal plugin | Quick in-place review of a document open in Word |
